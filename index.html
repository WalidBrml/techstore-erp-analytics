About the Project

From ERP transactions to business insights

TechStore Algeria is a simulated retail business created as a practical Data Analytics and Business Intelligence portfolio project. Purchasing, sales, inventory and accounting transactions were generated in ERPNext and analyzed through custom SQL reports.

The original ERPNext implementation contains seven custom SQL reports, eight financial KPIs and six analytical charts. Financial results were reconciled against General Ledger, Profit and Loss, Balance Sheet and Stock Balance reports.

This public demonstration uses a validated snapshot of the simulated dataset. It is not connected to a live ERPNext server or production database.
